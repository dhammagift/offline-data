# The Mahāsaṅgīti against all other editions

See [1-errors-in-sc-ms.md](1-errors-in-sc-ms.md) for the errors SC made in copying its source, the editions and
the method.

In these places SC follows its source faithfully: SC and the printed Mahāsaṅgīti (World Tipiṭaka 2005) read
alike, and every other edition that has the passage (at least three of Buddha Jayanti `bj`, Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana
`cck`, Syāmaraṭṭha `sya-all`, PTS `pts`) reads otherwise, all in the same way. These are readings of the
Mahāsaṅgīti edition itself, not errors of SC. Many look like misprints or normalizations of that edition
(`thinamiddha` for `thīnamiddha`, `-anupassanañāṇaṁ` for `-anupassanā ñāṇaṁ`); some may be deliberate editorial
choices. They are listed so that they can be checked and, where SC agrees, recorded in `variant/pli/ms`.
"SC variant" marks the 9 places where SC already gives this reading in its own variants.
An empty reading means the other editions do not have these words.
