# Bhikkhu Bodhi's translations (`*_translation-en-bodhi.json`)

Only the suttas that SuttaCentral publishes by Bhikkhu Bodhi, under the same terms. The text is verbatim; it is only
segmented to the Mahāsaṅgīti lines (the line-by-line arrangement of s.4nt.org, mapped to the segment ids by the Pali),
and each sutta's title is Bhikkhu Bodhi's.
