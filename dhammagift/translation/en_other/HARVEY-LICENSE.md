# Peter Harvey's translation (`*_translation-en-harvey.json`)

Peter Harvey, "Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta: The Discourse on the Setting in Motion of the Wheel (of Vision) of the Basic
Pattern: the Four True Realities for the Spiritually Ennobled Ones" (SN 56.11), translated from the Pali. Second edition
©2009 Peter Harvey; Access to Insight (BCBS Edition), 2 November 2013,
https://www.accesstoinsight.org/tipitaka/sn/sn56/sn56.011.harv.html

Derived from that source document: the text is verbatim, only cut onto the Mahāsaṅgīti lines (where he sums up the
second to fourth realities in one sentence, its three quoted parts go on the three lines; 12.4 and 12.11, which he does
not repeat, stay empty). His glossary and commentary are left out; read them at the link above.
